# TEAL-16

## The 16th International Workshop on Theoretical East Asian Linguistics (TEAL-16)

I am engaged in organizing the [16th International Workshop on Theoretical East Asian Linguistics (TEAL-16)](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/events/2027/06/theoretical-east-asian-linguistics--16th), which will take place in-person on June 10–12, 2027 at Leiden University, The Netherlands.

The Workshop on Theoretical East Asian Linguistics (TEAL) was first launched in 1990 by the Department of Linguistics at the University of California, Irvine, as a mid-sized workshop intended to promote research in East Asian linguistics. Since 2002, the TEAL Workshop has become an important international forum for presenting new theories and exchanging novel ideas concerning East Asian languages and their implications for linguistic theory.

### Call for Papers
We invite abstracts for 20-minute talks (plus 10 minutes for discussion) and posters on any theoretical or formal aspect of East Asian and Southeast Asian languages, including but not limited to syntax, semantics, phonology, morphology, language acquisition, and psycholinguistics. We particularly welcome work that addresses broader questions in linguistic theory based on evidence from East Asian and Southeast Asian languages.

### Submission Guidelines
*	Abstracts must be written in English and must not exceed two single-spaced A4 pages, including data and references. Abstracts must have 1-inch margins on all sides, set in 12pt Times New Roman font.
*	Abstracts and metadata must not reveal the identity of the author(s) in any way.
*	Abstracts should not present work that has already been published or accepted for publication.
*	Each author may submit at most two abstracts, with at most one being single-authored.
*	Abstracts must be submitted in PDF format via EasyAbs at https://easyabs.linguistlist.org/conference/TEAL-16/. Please name the file according to the first five words in the abstract, e.g. an-abstract-about-theoretical-linguistics.pdf. Please indicate whether your abstract is for a talk or for a poster.

For questions, please write to teal2027@hum.leidenuniv.nl.
